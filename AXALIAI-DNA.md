# Axaliai — The DNA of AI

## Identity

**Axaliai is the DNA of AI:** the development, interface, routing, and interoperability layer through which XParallel can express and connect intelligence.

This is an architectural metaphor, not a claim that Axaliai is biological DNA.

## Six functions

- **Architecture** — defines reusable AI components and their relationships.
- **eXchange** — connects intelligence, data, agents, services, and applications.
- **Access** — provides the authorized doorway into XParallel.
- **Logic** — routes requests and separates asking, understanding, and building.
- **Intelligence** — provides a model/agent integration boundary rather than depending on one model.
- **Application** — turns the infrastructure into usable experiences through Axaliai.

## XParallel relationship

XParallel is the infrastructure/ecosystem. Axaliai is its first development-layer client and its conversational gateway. The XParallel helix represents parallel strands of intelligence and infrastructure connected by X-links.

## Implementation direction

The current GitHub prototype implements the beginning of this model through the XParallel API, knowledge store, intent router, controlled external connectors, and execution-agent planning boundary.

The production target is **Axaliai.com → authenticated Axaliai gateway → XParallel → authorized information, models, agents, and services**.

## Principle

> Axaliai does not have to be the whole AI universe. It is the layer that lets the AI universe connect, develop, and express itself.
